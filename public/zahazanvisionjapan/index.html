1. Objective & Scope Overview

Zahazan Travel & Tours Sdn. Bhd. (Malaysia) is evaluating the launch of a dedicated 7-Day / 6-Night (7D6N) Muslim-Friendly Japan Tour Series for 2027 departures. Building upon Japan Destinations’ existing Muslim-Friendly Tour Package framework, this RFQ defines the ground specifications, halal compliance standards, and B2B pricing tiers required to finalize retail costing and airline seat allocations.

2. Route Options & Core Inclusions

  • Route Option A (Open-Jaw — Preferred): Arrival Tokyo (NRT/HND) → Mt. Fuji / Lake Yamanaka → Kyoto (via Shinkansen) → Nara → Departure Osaka (KIX).
  • Route Option B (Round-Trip Tokyo): Same route with return Shinkansen/transfer to Tokyo (NRT/HND) for departure (please quote separately if applicable).
  • Accommodation: 6 nights at 4-star (or equivalent) Muslim-friendly hotels equipped with in-room Qibla direction indicators and prayer mats.
  • Full-Board Dining: 6 Breakfasts, 6 Lunches, and 6 Dinners (100% pork-free and alcohol-free).
  • Transport & Rail: Private chartered coach with licensed driver throughout, plus reserved Shinkansen tickets (Tokyo – Kyoto).
  • Sightseeing & Guide: All attraction admissions as per sample itinerary and a dedicated licensed tour guide.

3. Mandatory Malaysian Market Requirements

  1. Halal & Dietary Transparency: All meals must be strictly free of pork, lard, and alcohol (including cooking seasonings such as mirin and soy sauce). Please indicate per meal whether the venue is Halal-certified, Muslim-owned, or Muslim-friendly (seafood/vegetarian/certified halal meat).
  2. Daily Prayer Facilitation: Dedicated prayer stops must be integrated into the daily itinerary across Tokyo (e.g., Tokyo Camii), Mt. Fuji, Kyoto, Nara, and Osaka without rushing sightseeing schedules.
  3. Tour Leader FOC Policy: Net quotes must include 1 Free-of-Charge (FOC) slot (covering accommodation, meals, Shinkansen, and admissions) for an accompanying Malaysian Tour Leader / Mutawwif.
  4. Guide Language Options: Please quote separately for a Malay-speaking or Indonesian-speaking licensed guide versus an English-speaking licensed guide.
