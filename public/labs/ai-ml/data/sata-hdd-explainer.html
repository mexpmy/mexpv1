Production notes

  • Frame: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Action is kept inside a 6% side margin; the lower 22% is reserved for the caption band and the logo, which sits bottom-right at 13% frame width throughout.
  • Colour logic: the frame is monochrome graphite and platinum. Only two hues appear, and each one means something — blue is magnetisation and magnetic field, orange is heat. Orange is held back until HAMR at 2:35 so it reads as an event.
  • Typography: Archivo for titles and narration captions; IBM Plex Mono reserved for measured quantities, so numbers are visually separable from prose.
  • Narration: approximately 470 words at 135–145 wpm, unhurried. Every figure spoken is also on screen for at least two seconds.
  • Accuracy notes: early longitudinal media used cobalt-chromium-tantalum; current perpendicular media is a cobalt-chromium-platinum-oxide granular film, and the script says both. Modern read heads are tunnelling magnetoresistive with an MgO barrier — GMR is named as the discovery that started the lineage, not as what is in the drive today.
